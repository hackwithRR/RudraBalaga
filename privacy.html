Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What we collect

  • Account details: name, phone number and email used at registration / login.
  • RSVP data: your attendance responses, transport mode and bus pickup selections.
  • Payment proofs: screenshots and UTR/reference numbers you upload for event fees and donations.
  • Technical data: notification tokens (for push announcements) and basic app usage needed to run the service.

2. How we use it

  • To manage events, attendance lists, bus seating and payment verification.
  • To send you announcements, reminders and payment-status updates.
  • Your payment proof is visible only to Group administrators for verification.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing.
  • We do not store your UPI PIN, bank passwords or card details — payments happen entirely in your UPI/bank app.

4. Storage & security

Data is stored on Google Firebase (Firestore) with access restricted to authorised administrators. We keep payment proofs only as long as needed for verification and record-keeping.

5. Your choices

  • You may request correction or deletion of your account data by contacting the administrators.
  • You can disable notifications at any time from your device or browser settings.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, please contact the Group administrators:

  • Email: Founder@buildandguild.space
  • Support: get.support@guildandbuild.space
  • Phone: 9019897682

7. Platform ownership

The Platform software, including its code, design, branding elements and related materials, is owned and managed by Build & Guild. Rudra Balaga uses the Platform for its community activities. Build & Guild manages the software infrastructure and does not sell personal data for marketing.
